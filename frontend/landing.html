Root Causes of Portal Rejection

Small document mistakes can become big submission problems.

Administrative databases lack human discretionary judgement. When automated parsers detect string variance or formatting flags, applications are systematically archived without human intervention.

01

Identity & Name Discrepancies

Subtle spelling variants—such as truncated middle initials, patronymic re-ordering, or spacing discrepancies between Aadhaar and secondary marksheets—frequently trigger algorithmic identity rejections.

Failure Rate: High · Algorithmic Reject
02

Income Threshold Violations

Submitting certificates from an outdated assessment year or presenting gross income exceeding statutory caps (such as the standard ₹2,50,000 threshold) invalidates financial aid qualification immediately.

Failure Rate: Absolute · Statutory Disqualification
03

File & Format Non-Compliance

Illegible seal impressions, unindexed multipage PDF files, and scans exceeding the standard 2048 KB portal limit cause silent upload timeouts or automated gatekeeping validation errors.

Failure Rate: Moderate · Server Drop / Error 413

Statutory Implication

State scholarship portals require exact canonical character matching against Aadhaar UIDAI records. Abbreviated initialisms fail automated deduplication algorithms.

Actionable Fix

Submit an affidavit of identity alongside the application, or upload your secondary certificate showing the expanded surname prior to statutory submission.

AMBER · Review

Document Value: ₹2,60,000 / annum

Statutory Implication

Applications exceeding the statutory income ceiling are filtered automatically by the verification server before manual scrutiny by the welfare department.

Actionable Fix

Verify whether your latest Revenue Department certificate reflects deductions for agricultural losses or if a newer assessment year certificate is available.
